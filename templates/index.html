What this is

      
A benchmark and demonstration harness for MIND INCEPTION (Harvested
      Adversarial Neural Guidance): an attack that plants a borrowed reasoning trace in an
      artifact so a large reasoning model adopts it and flips a security verdict to CLEAN.

      
The lab measures how far a battery of defenses moves the attack on three axes:
      attack success rate (ASR), benign false-positive cost (FP), and detection utility.
      Defense families:

      
    
        
  • surface — comment stripping, prose removal (the paper's Table 7).
    • 
        
  • structural — sandwich, spotlighting, data-marking, encoding, instruction hierarchy (the paper's untested frontier).
    • 
        
  • detector — Llama-Guard & a planted-chain-of-thought classifier as pre-filters.
    • 
        
  • architectural — N-model vote and a comment-blind signature cross-check; a defense-in-depth stack.
    • 
      

      
Samples are public webshells already flagged by conventional tools, plus benign code.
      Planted traces shipped here are neutralized demonstrations; operational traces are withheld.
